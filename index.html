Meet Asha

I started Kanta Saree in 2020 with a small shelf of sarees and a big love for fabric. My mother and aunts always said a good saree makes you stand a little taller, and I wanted every woman in our neighbourhood to feel that.

I still pick every piece myself. If I would not wear it to a family function, it does not come to the shop. The Chinese party wear range became our favourite because it looks rich but does not cost a fortune.

Come to Ramanand Nagar, sit down, have some chai and touch the fabric before you decide.
